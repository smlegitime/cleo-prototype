# Maintenance Guide: From-Scratch Baking Community Standards

## What Your Labeler Does

**From-Scratch Baking Community Standards** applies two labels:

1. **From-Scratch Ethos** (visible badge) — Marks posts that use specific from-scratch baking vocabulary
2. **Product Spam** (hidden behind click-through) — Flags product spam from brand-new accounts

## How Classification Works

Your labeler decides when to apply labels using **three signal types only**:
- **Specific words or phrases** (e.g., "wild yeast", "link in bio")
- **Text patterns** (e.g., common misspellings or spacing tricks around those words)
- **Account traits** (e.g., follower count under 30)

It **cannot** judge tone, sarcasm, meaning, or context. It matches the signals above independently — there's no "keyword AND link" logic beyond what's explicitly configured.

## Current Rules

### From-Scratch Ethos
Fires when a post contains **any** of these phrases:
- "wild yeast"
- "naturally leavened"
- "home-milled"
- "fresh-milled flour"
- "levain"
- "no commercial yeast"
- "100% whole grain"

**Why these?** Deliberately narrow vocabulary that marks real from-scratch culture, not just generic "sourdough" or "starter" mentions.

**Expected behavior:** Under-marks rather than over-marks. A deserving post might not get the badge if it doesn't use this specific language — that's intentional to avoid false positives.

### Product Spam
Fires when **BOTH** conditions are met:
- Post contains a sales phrase: "link in bio", DM-for-sales wording, discount percentages (e.g., "60% off"), or "limited stock"
- **AND** author has fewer than 30 followers

**Why both?** Established members mentioning a link won't be caught (they have followers). Brand-new accounts posting without sales language won't be caught either. Only the combination flags spam.

**Expected behavior:** May see zero hits in testing if no spam accounts are active. Will activate when dropshipper accounts appear.

## Common Maintenance Tasks

### Adding From-Scratch Vocabulary
If your community adopts new terminology (e.g., "stone-ground", "ancient grains"), add it to the **from_scratch_ethos** phrase list. Keep it specific — avoid broad terms that appear in commercial baking posts.

### Adjusting the Follower Threshold
Currently set to **under 30 followers**. If you're catching legitimate new members:
- Raise the threshold (e.g., under 20 or under 10)
- Or tighten the sales phrase list to more obvious spam language

If spam accounts are slipping through:
- Lower the threshold slightly (but be cautious — real newcomers exist)
- Or add new sales phrases you're seeing

### Adding Sales Phrases
If spammers start using new language (e.g., "shop my link", "exclusive offer"), add those phrases to the **product_spam** rule. They'll still need the low-follower-count condition to fire.

## What This Labeler Cannot Do

**Out of scope for this version:**
- Analyzing links or the sites a post links to
- Maintaining "known offender" or MLM account lists (account traits are limited to metadata like follower count)
- Judging tone, meaning, sarcasm, or whether someone is mocking vs. endorsing something
- Rules that only fire when multiple signals co-occur in complex ways (each signal matches independently)

If you need any of these, frame them as future improvements and work with the closest words/phrase or account-trait approximation that does work now.

## Monitoring Performance

**Check regularly:**
- Are **from_scratch_ethos** badges appearing on the right posts? If too many generic posts are getting marked, tighten the vocabulary list.
- Is **product_spam** catching the dropshippers? If spam is slipping through, review what language they're using and add it.
- Are real members getting caught by **product_spam**? If so, raise the follower threshold.
